Backed by a 10-year structural warranty

As a 2-10 Home Buyers Warranty (2-10 HBW) builder member, every Grace Allen Homes property comes with structural protection from one of the country's leading new-home warranty providers — because building your dream home shouldn't end your peace of mind.

Day 1Coverage begins at closing
W

Workmanship Warranty

Covers the fit, finish, and materials of your home — things like cabinets, countertops, flooring, trim, paint, doors, and roofing — so the details hold up the way they should.

D

Distribution Systems Warranty

Covers what's working behind your walls: supply and waste piping, ductwork, and electrical wiring, so the systems that keep your home running are protected too.

S

Structural Warranty

Covers the load-bearing bones of your home — framing, foundation, beams, and floor systems — for a full 10 years from closing.
